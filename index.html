04 Why Wildflour

Made for the craft, not the conveyor belt.

01

Stone-ground flour, every batch.

We mill our own flour from locally sourced grains. No bleached, no bromated — just real wheat that tastes like wheat.

02

72-hour croissant dough.

Our laminated dough rests three full days before it hits the oven. The result? Layers so light they practically float off the plate.

03

Real butter, not margarine.

European-style butter with 82% fat content. It costs more, but you can taste the difference in every flake, every crumb.

04

Small batches, big flavour.

We bake in small batches throughout the day so every loaf comes out warm. No day-old bread, no reheated pastries.

05

Zero preservatives, ever.

If it's not in our pantry, it's not in our bread. Our sourdough stays fresh for days naturally — no chemicals required.

06

Community, not customers.

We know our regulars by name and by order. If we haven't met you yet, we're saving you a warm loaf.

07 FAQ

Quick answers, before you bite in.

Do I need to pre-order, or can I walk in?

Both work. Walk-ins get first pick of the daily batch — we bake all morning. For custom cakes and large orders (10+ pastries), we recommend ordering via WhatsApp a day ahead.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes. We offer gluten-free sourdough and a rotating selection of vegan pastries (ask about our banana bread and chocolate cookies). Please let us know 24 hours ahead for gluten-free orders so we can prep a fresh batch.

Can I customise my cake or order?

Absolutely. Custom messages on cakes, alternate fillings, dietary swaps — just tell us what you need on WhatsApp and we'll make it happen. Custom orders require 48 hours' notice.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Free delivery within 5 km for orders above ₹1,000. A nominal fee of ₹50 applies for smaller orders. Delivery is available 8am-6pm daily. Order via WhatsApp with your address.

What time do you bake fresh?

Our ovens fire up at 4 AM every morning. First batch of bread comes out by 6 AM, pastries by 7 AM, and fresh cakes by 9 AM. We bake in small batches throughout the day so everything stays warm.

What if I just want to try a little bit?

Grab a single cookie or a croissant for ₹150 at the counter. If you love it (you will), come back for a full loaf. No membership, no minimum — just good bread.
